Theme Rooms

Forms that hold
different kinds of memory.

DAMOLDA begins with three forms: Shell, Sheets, and Stone. They are arranged into two rooms — one still and grounded, one layered and in motion.

Sessile Theme Room

Still forms
that stay.

Shell and Stone are quiet, anchored forms. They are made for memories that need weight, pause, and a fixed place in the room.

Room 01

Shell

A hollow frame for beginnings, first attempts, seasons, and memories still becoming.

Opening Series

Room 02

Stone

A grounded form for weight, silence, touch, and memories that should not feel fragile.

Weight Series

Dynamic Theme Room

Layered surfaces
that move.

Sheets is the active room. It is made for arranging, overlapping, pressing, and changing the way small fragments meet each other.

Room 03

Sheets

Layered surfaces for fragments, colors, pressed moments, and the small evidence of a day.

Layer Series

DAMOLDA collections are built as theme rooms: each form is a different way to keep what matters.
